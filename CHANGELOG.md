## 0.1.0 – 2026-11-21

The first alpha: the hall and one game.

### Added

- The hall: a neon arcade at night, one cabinet per game. A ready game plays itself on its cabinet's screen (attract mode, drawn by the game's own code), the rest say "Soon". Without JavaScript the cabinets are plain links.
- Fuse Box, a logic puzzle: find the blown fuses from the numbers on the good ones, then fix the panel. Three levels, the first test is always safe, tag with a right click, a long press, the T key or Tag mode, and play it all with a keyboard.
- Hall of fame: the top 10 times per level with three-letter initials and dates, kept in this browser only.
- Sound effects made in code (no audio files), with a switch in the footer.
- Checks: unit tests, a privacy scan, a size budget, and a layout check in a real browser at 320, 360, 768 and 1280 px.
