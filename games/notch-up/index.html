Notch Up

Drop each floor on the one below. Whatever hangs over is cut off.

How to play

Build the Notchup tower as high as it goes. A new floor slides back and forth above the top; drop it on the floor below.

  • Tap or click anywhere on the tower, or press Space or Enter, to drop the floor.
  • Whatever hangs over the edge is cut off, so the next floor is narrower.
  • A drop that lines up (almost) exactly is perfect and keeps the whole width. From the third perfect in a row, each perfect wins some width back.
  • A floor that misses the tower ends the run. The floors get a little faster as the tower grows.
  • Every tenth floor the logo notches up, and 10, 50 and 100 floors get a cheer and a helping hand: the crane builds that floor wider (at 100, back to full width), so the floors after it are wider too.
