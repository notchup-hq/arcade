How to play

Build the four stacks from ace to king, one suit each. Every deal can be won.

  • Tap a card to send it where it fits. Tap a card in a column to move it with the cards on top of it. Or drag.
  • Tap the draw pile to turn cards over. Tap it when it is empty to turn the discard pile back.
  • In a column, put a card on the next one up, in the other colour. Only a king goes in an empty column.
  • Undo takes back your last move. It is free, and it counts as a move.
  • Draw 1 turns one card at a time, Draw 3 turns three.
  • Your time is counted, but not shown until you win. The best time is the best score.
  • Keyboard: arrows move around the table, Enter does what a tap does. In a column, [ and ] choose how many cards. M picks cards up, then Enter on a place puts them down; Escape puts them back. U undoes.
