How to play

Every card has a twin. Turn two cards at a time and find all the pairs.

  • Tap or click a card to turn it. Turn a second one: if they match they stay up, if not they turn back down and you lose a chance.
  • A wrong guess uses up a chance: you have 4 on Easy, 5 on Medium and 6 on Hard, shown as lamps. Every pair you find lights one lamp back.
  • Find every pair and you get lamps back (one on Easy and Medium, two on Hard), then the next round starts with a fresh deck. The run goes on until all the lamps are out.
  • Pairs found one after another score more, and so do the lamps left when you clear a round.
  • The faces are letters and little pictures from the other games: the logo, a bulb, a fuse and a floor of the tower, and bugs that look alike: a beetle, a ladybird, a spider and a butterfly.
  • On a keyboard, the arrow keys move around the cards and Enter or Space turns one.
