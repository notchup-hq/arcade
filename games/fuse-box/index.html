How to play

Every number on a lit fuse says how many of the eight fuses around it are blown. Test every good fuse to fix the panel; test a blown one and it sparks.

  • Tap or click a fuse to test it. The first one is always safe.
  • Tag a fuse you think is blown: switch the buttons under the panel from Test to Tag, or long press, right click, or press T.
  • When a number has all its blown fuses tagged, test it again to test the rest around it.
  • On a keyboard, the arrow keys move around the panel and Enter tests.
