    How to play

    Each wave asks one times-table question. A bug crawls in along every lane, each carrying a number. Zap the one with the right answer before it reaches the logo.

    • Tap a bug, or anywhere in its lane. On a keyboard, aim the turret and fire: the arrow keys turn it (two together, like up and right, aim at the corner), Q, E, Z, C and the number pad work too, and Space or Enter fires.
    • A wrong zap pushes that bug back, nudges the other bugs closer, takes off what the answer is worth (25, then 50, then 75), and ends your streak. A third wrong zap in one wave puts out a light, so guessing every lane does not pay.
    • If the right bug gets through, a light goes out. Three lights out ends the run.
    • Hard also asks for a missing number, like 7 × ? = 56: the bugs carry the factors.
    • A fact you miss comes back a few questions later, and the list of misses shows at the end. This browser remembers your weak facts for next time; nothing leaves it.
    • Score: the number in the corner is what the right answer is worth now. It starts at 200 and counts down as the bug crawls in, so quick answers score more. It is multiplied by your streak: ×2 at 3 in a row, ×3 at 6, ×4 at 10.
