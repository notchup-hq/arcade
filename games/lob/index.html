How to play

Lob glowing bulbs at the billboard on the other roof, the sign inside the gold brackets. The players are not the target. Three hits on the sign and it goes dark. The wind changes every throw: the smoke leans with it and the number says how strong.

  • Drag back from your roof and let go. Pulling further throws harder, pulling lower throws higher.
  • Or use − and + for the angle and the power, then press Throw.
  • On a keyboard: up and down for the angle, left and right for the power, Space to throw.
  • Solo is a run: beat one rival after another. Each aims a little better, with a little more wind. The run ends on your first loss. Beat a rival in fewer throws for a bonus.
  • Two players share one device and take turns. It has no scores.
